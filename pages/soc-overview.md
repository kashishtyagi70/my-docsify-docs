# SOC Overview

## What is a SOC?

A Security Operations Center monitors and responds to cyber threats.

---

## Core Components

- SIEM
- EDR/XDR
- IDS/IPS
- Threat Intelligence
- Firewalls

---

## SOC Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 Analyst | Alert triage |
| Tier 2 Analyst | Investigation |
| Threat Hunter | Proactive hunting |
| Incident Responder | Containment |
